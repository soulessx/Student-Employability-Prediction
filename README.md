# Student-Employability-Prediction

A proposed system that explores how machine learning can help university students understand employment and identify skills for further development.

## Problem statement
It can be challenging for students who are seeking employment to determine their areas of improvement. If they don't have a system of reviewing their assessment, they might not know their strengths and weaknesses.

Previous research has explored machine learning for employability prediction (Vo et al., 2023) used mock interview assessment data to classify student employability, while (Mansor & Othman, 2024) investigated employability prediction among Malaysian community college graduates. However, employability classification alone may not provide a clear overview of a student's performance across individual assessment attributes. Students may benefit from visual summaries that make these attributes easier to understand.

Therefore, this project aims to develop a system that combines machine learning-based employability classification with skills analytics. The system will predict the employability category defined by the dataset and display assessment attributes to highlight potential areas for improvement.
